About Me

Paid advertising that goes beyond clicks. Built for growth.

I'm a Google Ads and Performance Marketing Specialist with 3.2+ years of PPC experience. I build and optimize Google, Meta and YouTube advertising campaigns that generate qualified leads, increase sales and help businesses scale profitably.
